# Playbook — the standard starting point

This is the playbook every instance starts from. It holds the editing
techniques the director knows. It carries nothing channel-specific: the
owner's wording, style and lessons live in the instance's own settings and
notebook.

## The editing techniques

- The brief is the contract: its length, its order and its pacing.
- A long take is several moments: cover its arc, not two neighbours from one end.
- Cut on the shot's best moment, not its start or its end out of habit.
- A move and the hold it lands on are one phrase.
- Photos are slots: a still can hold a moment better than a clip.
- Do not pad: fewer slots beat weak shots.

## The editing grammar

The grammar — the shot window, the landing floor, the hero cap, the same-take
rule, the similarity rule and the pacing limits — lives in `docs/RULES.md` and
in the role prompts. This playbook does not restate it.
