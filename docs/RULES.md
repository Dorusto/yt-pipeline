# RULES.md — rules in force

Current state, one line per rule, with the `DECISIONS.md` entry date it comes from. `DECISIONS.md` stays the history; when a decision changes a rule, update this file in the same commit (2026-10-14). Agents read this file plus the last 10 `DECISIONS.md` entries, not the whole log.

## Editing grammar

- Analysis tools label, never delete: one take can hold several intentional shots; the director decides what becomes a slot. — 2026-10-12
- Geometry `accidental` / `setup` / `teardown` are never slots, whatever the shotlog says; a vision verdict may relabel context but never re-admits the segment. — 2026-10-13 / 2026-10-14
- A move and the hold it lands on form one phrase: keep the move through its landing or use only the hold; never cut out of a move and into its own landing (no jump cut). — 2026-10-13
- Phrase window: the end cut lands inside the hold (`out >= landing_start + 0.75 s`); the head trims to the pacing target but never before the move's handle-in; a phrase never exceeds its target. — 2026-10-13
- Phrase score is the move's score; the phrase wins on a tie; the hold alone wins exactly when the hold is the better shot on its own. — 2026-10-13
- Two slots from one take need a different take between them unless they are source-contiguous (±0.5 s); contiguous neighbours merge into one slot. — 2026-10-13
- One candidate (segment or content beat) contributes at most one slot; a long take may contribute several beats with a different take between them, or back to back when both are camera moves cut forward at least 8 s apart (`common.moves.is_jump_cut`; e.g. 0072 tunnel approach then exit); static framings of one take never sit back to back. Within one candidate a higher-scoring blocked window replaces the conflicting slot (`same-take upgrade`). — 2026-10-13 / 2026-10-14 / 2026-10-15 (D2-14)
- No two visually similar segments in one edit unless the brief asks for it; never back to back, even with `--allow-similar`. — 2026-10-13
- Hero shots and complete semantic moves (orbit, reveal, tracking, pov, travel) keep their whole steady range up to the cap; pacing shortening applies only to ordinary shots; a static hero hold is a static shot (see the length policy below). — 2026-10-13 / 2026-10-15
- Length policy (D2-11, speed ramp optional since D2-12): static shots, holds and photos stay within `slot_max`; a camera move (role move/transition_in/transition_out or important, label not static/minor_motion) keeps as much of its window as fits; at most 15 % of the slots, rounded (minimum 1, best scores first) may run past `slot_max` on screen, up to `hero_max`; the policy runs before the beat snap and the snap never breaks a slot's cap. Normal speed is the default: `middle_speed` is 1.0 everywhere unless `--speed-ramp` is given (director, llm_director, recap; the web Upload and Re-edit forms have a Speed ramp select, Auto = off), and then the middle half of a move may play up to 1.2x (head and tail quarters real time). `meta.speed_ramp` records the setting and a change re-runs the edit. — 2026-10-15 / 2026-10-15 (D2-12)
- On-screen length of a slot is `0.25L + 0.5L/s + 0.25L` (`common/moves.slot_screen_length`); equal to the source length when `middle_speed` is 1; every tool that needs a slot's length uses it; Kdenlive export stays real time and warns. — 2026-10-15
- An edit must not miss an eligible hero when one exists (golden invariant, tested on fixtures). — 2026-10-13 / 2026-10-14
- Important-shot cap: default 6 s (was 8 s). A cut that has to be shorter than its window is centred on the shot's peak (best moment) and lies inside the steady range, never on aimless movement after it; a window that fits whole is kept whole. — 2026-10-14 / 2026-10-15 (D2-12)
- Peak-centred cuts (D2-12): a move longer than its allowed on-screen length is trimmed around its best moment, not its start or end. Peak source priority: (a) `peak_s` from describe (validated to lie inside the segment or beat, carried by index into `shotlog.json`), (b) the OpenCV signal in `motion/` (time of the maximum smoothed `scene_activity` inside the window, only when it stands out from the clip median), (c) the middle of the window. The window is centred on the peak and clamped inside the candidate window (`common/peak.py`, used by both directors). A landing phrase still ends at or after its landing floor, and that rule wins over the peak (the director warns when the peak falls outside the cut). Slots record `peak_s`, `peak_source` (vision|motion|middle) and `centred`; the llm validator expects trimmed cuts centred on `peak_s` within 0.3 s. — 2026-10-15 (D2-12)
- Ordinary slot length comes from the brief's pacing (calm vs energetic) with a min/max; cut inside the steady range. — 2026-10-13
- Pacing table (D2-7): shot length scales with the target length — 20 s → 8–10 shots of 1.5–2.5 s, 90 s → 20–25 shots of 2–5 s, 3 min → up to 6 s; hero and semantic-move slots stay the longest but shrink in proportion (hero cap `min(6, slot_target × 1.75)`); `--min-slot-s`/`--max-slot-s`/`--important-max-s` are overrides of the table. — 2026-10-15
- Time-overlapping shots (>50% of the shorter) keep the higher score; the loser is suppressed. — 2026-10-13
- Long segments (>20 s) split into content beats: `describe` samples a frame every ~5 s, marks scene changes; each beat becomes its own candidate. — 2026-10-14
- The screenwriter's menu is the cuttable pool: only windows the candidacy rule above allows. — 2026-10-13
- `edit.json` is the edit list and the only source of truth; render, preview, graphics and Kdenlive export are adapters that read it. — 2026-10-13
- Mode B paired slots (S56): two consecutive video slots sharing one `id` are two angles of one cut window (screen first, then camera); `export_filmcraft.py` places them at one shared timeline start with one audio clip (the non-screen angle) and one marker per pair. `render.py`, `subs_from_edit.py` and the web app still treat slots sequentially — in this slice a Mode B rough cut feeds the FilmCraft export and the eval only. — 2026-10-21
- Mode B cut rules (S56): pauses over 0.6 s are cut with a 0.12 s handle each side, standalone fillers from the config file are dropped, a restarted sentence is cut keeping the last take (thresholds in scripts/rough_cut_config_example.yaml, tuned on the finished stretch of the reference eval with `--until-s 150`; rerun the eval when the reference edit is final); every cut run is a rejected decisions[] entry and the kept window's reason names the cut before it. — 2026-10-21
- `cut_review.yaml` (workspace root, machine-owned, written by `serve.py` from the Cuts-tab taps) is the phone cut review file: `rough_cut.py --review` keeps a `keep` run in the edit (reason `kept by review`) and `eval_rough_cut.py --review` reads its rows as extra ground truth that wins over the reference inside the reviewed span. — 2026-10-21 (S57 lot 1)
- `chapters.json` (`schemas/chapters.schema.json`) is the chapter source of truth from `chapters.py` (role `metadata`, first chapter at 0, non-decreasing, titles ≤ 40); `export_filmcraft.py --chapters` places one Green section marker per chapter, `metadata.py --chapters` and `publish.py --chapters` use the file instead of asking the model again, and `mode_b.py` runs the step. — 2026-10-21 (S57 lot 2)
- `shorts_candidates.json` (`schemas/shorts_candidates.schema.json`) is the ranked Shorts list from `shorts_rank.py` (standalone sentence-aligned 20–60 s spans inside one chapter that start at or after 60 s of the edit — the intro block never becomes a candidate, whatever the span's end —, no two written candidates overlapping by more than 20 %, every candidate carrying `title` (max 60 characters, hook first) and `description` (2 sentences, no payoff spoilers; a description that is not exactly 2 sentences is a warning in the output, never a rejection) from the same LLM pass, score `(hook + payoff)/2` as the single rank, top 5, a `layout: face|split` per candidate from `--layout-policy` auto/on/off — config `shorts_layout`, `--layout` the hard override; `split` only for a camera+screen session whose screen shows during the candidate, camera-only and Mode A always `face`); `shorts_generator.py --candidates` cuts them and `export_filmcraft.py --shorts-candidates` marks the top 3 with Mango. — 2026-10-21 (S57 lot 3) / 2026-10-21 (S59 follow-up / S59 follow-up 2) / 2026-10-22 (S60 lot 7) / 2026-10-22 (S60 lot 7b)
- The directors record a reason for every candidate they do not use (near-duplicate of X, defect, setup/teardown, low score, did not fit the length) in `edit.json` `decisions[]`, with the short id (`0071/s2`) also on the used slots (`shot_key`); `explain.html` renders that list. — 2026-10-15
- `edit.json` records the music track (`audio.music`) and the ambient level under it (`audio.ambient_under_music_db`) whenever they come from flags, so the render's mix is reproducible from the edit alone. — 2026-10-15

## Defaults and thresholds

- Motion floors: pan/tilt 0.03 fw/s, roll 5 deg/s, dolly 0.05 log/s plus 70% sign consistency; split on dominant-type change (0.6 s hysteresis, 0.4 s short-run merge). — 2026-10-12
- Parallax = `min(1, residual_norm / 0.02)`; ≥ 0.5 relabels lateral moves to `truck_*`; centred subject (centre-box residual ≤ 0.35 × periphery) proposes `orbit_candidate`. Precedence `travel_*` > `orbit_candidate` > `truck_*` > `pan_*`. — 2026-10-13
- `platform_bob` is an annotation (amplitude ≥ 0.008 of frame height, period 1.5–8 s), never a label; `travel_*` upgrades sustained smooth scale runs ≥ 8 s. — 2026-10-13
- Horizon: only a dominant long (≥ 25% width) near-horizontal (≤ 15°) line with angle MAD ≤ 3°, else `null`; the defect fires above 5° (= eval tolerance). — 2026-10-13
- Scene activity from residual local flow on the upper 70% of the frame (scene band); event noise floor 0.001, event start 0.001 above the floor. — 2026-10-13
- `accidental`: unstable run ≥ 5 s or ≥ 70% of the clip; whole-clip confidence 0.65, run 0.6; healthy travel shake 0.007–0.012 is stable content. By default the run rule covers junk frames only (obstruction, near-uniform frame, static low-detail frame): a long shaky run keeps its move or hold role and reaches the vision model and the director with its shake defect; `YT_PIPELINE_SHAKY=drop` restores the old upgrade to `accidental`. — 2026-10-13 / 2026-10-23 (V010 lot fix1)
- Quality segments: head/tail are before the first / after the last content segment; slivers merge into a neighbour with `end_s > start_s`; a `static` label is never role `move`. — 2026-10-13
- Shotlog score = `0.55·vision + 0.45·labels`; vision = interest/100 (+0.15 hero); missing vision is a neutral 0.5 prior; the verdict wins on `uncertain` segments. — 2026-10-13
- Confident `accidental`/`setup`/`teardown` are `eligible: false` in the shotlog. — 2026-10-13
- Similarity: MobileNetV2 ONNX through `cv2.dnn` (hist backend offline); "similar" starts at 0.90 and stays a flag. — 2026-10-13
- Eval scoring: interval overlap (full credit exact, 0.5 geometric look-alike), horizon tolerance 5°, events one-to-one F1 within ±3 s. — 2026-10-13
- `describe`: frames at OpenCV scene changes and motion phase boundaries, tiled 6-9 per mosaic image with burned-in timestamps (one image per group, `--frame-mode legacy` keeps the fixed-position separate frames), timestamps + motion label in the facts, `long: yes|no` keys the beats, one-line summary (≤ 12 words) + tags, retry once on unparseable output, `max_tokens` 8000 for reasoning models. — 2026-10-13 / 2026-10-15
- Render: 720p preview from proxies, final from the originals at native size (`--originals-dir`), crossfade 0.3 s, frame-inclusive Kdenlive `out` (`out_s - 1/fps`); a slot with `middle_speed` renders as three trims (middle `setpts`/`atempo`), byte-for-byte as before when it is 1. — 2026-10-13 / 2026-10-15

## Tool exit codes

- One table for every tool (AUDIT F08): `0` success; `1` finished, but found something a human must look at (check-type tools, suspect results); `2` usage, input or config error, nothing ran (bad flags, missing input file, missing or broken config); `3` runtime failure after work started (external program, model call, disk). The four constants live in `scripts/common/exit_codes.py` (`EXIT_OK`, `EXIT_ATTENTION`, `EXIT_USAGE`, `EXIT_RUNTIME`) and every `main()` returns them. `scripts/mode_b.py` is the one exception and keeps its documented 0/1/2/3 meanings (all clear / suspect / incomplete / error). — 2026-10-23 (AUDIT lot 3b1)
- A tool reports `EXIT_USAGE` for the input and config problems it detects itself and `EXIT_RUNTIME` for failures of the work it started — including a called program that failed, whatever that program's own exit code was; a model answer that cannot be parsed is a model-call failure (`EXIT_RUNTIME`), and a missing `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` is a config error (`EXIT_USAGE`). — 2026-10-23 (AUDIT lot 3b1)
- Every tool with `--json` reports a failure as one `{"ok": false, "error": "...", "exit_code": N}` object on stdout (the human `[error]` line stays on stderr), through `scripts/common/errors.py` `report_error(error, exit_code, json_out, **extra)`, which returns the exit code. — 2026-10-23 (AUDIT lot 3b2, F24)

## Photos and music (D2-6)

- Photos are first-class candidates: ingest scans stills next to the videos (JPEG/HEIC, EXIF capture time via Pillow + pillow-heif), describe covers each photo with one frame, similarity dedups bursts, index writes them to the shotlog with `media_type: "photo"`. — 2026-10-14
- A photo describes as pseudo-segment `p1` (`0..0.04 s`) with `subject_position`; its labels score is a fixed clean-still prior 0.7 fused with vision as usual. — 2026-10-14
- Burst rule: photo shots linked by similarity and captured within 30 s of each other form a burst; only the best stays eligible (`suppressed_by` the winner). The best is a photo without a spoiling flaw (`eyes_closed`, `blur`, `motion_blur`, `obstructed` from describe's `photo_flaws`) first, then the highest score weighted by relative sharpness (Laplacian, 0.7-1.0 within the burst); a lone photo with a spoiling flaw is not eligible. — 2026-10-14 / 2026-10-15 (D2-15)
- Photo slots (`kind: "photo"`) last 2–4 s and carry a Ken Burns move chosen deterministically from `subject_position`: centre box → `zoom_in` on the subject; else `pan_left`/`pan_right` toward the subject; `zoom_out` only for close-ups. — 2026-10-14
- Ken Burns renders with ffmpeg `zoompan`; portrait photos sit on a blurred background (cover-scaled copy + boxblur), never black bars. — 2026-10-14
- Music: `render.py`/`recap.py` take `--music FILE`; the track is the bed, normalised to the loudness target, and the cleaned ambient bus stays audible `--ambient-under-music-db` (default 12) below it — never removed; a loud ambient event dips the music gently (sidechain, ratio 3); fade-out at the end; no music generation before D6. — 2026-10-14
- `--music auto` picks the track from the music library with `scripts/music_pick.py`: brief pace words set the BPM band and the energy target, the shot tags match the file name, `beats.py` measures BPM/energy; the choice and the beat grid land in `music_choice.json`/`beats.json`. The library folder has no personal default anywhere: `--library` (`music_pick.py`) / `--music-dir` (`recap.py`, `serve.py`), else `YT_PIPELINE_MUSIC_LIBRARY`, else `music.library` in the config, and with none set the tool stops with a usage error naming those three ways; docs and examples write `<music-library>`; tools only read the folder and never download music; `music_pick.py --list` reports path, duration, BPM and licence file status per track. — 2026-10-15 / 2026-10-22 (S60 lot 8)
